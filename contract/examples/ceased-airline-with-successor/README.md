# Ceased airline with successor

An airline that stopped operating and whose fleet moved to its successor.

- `al-old-example` ceased in 2019-10, has `successor_id: al-example-successor`, `fleet_count: 0`, empty `family_counts`, and **no** fleet file.
- It still appears in `airlines.json` and in search, so successor links and the time slider can reference it.
- Its former aircraft now fly for the successor with `previous_operator_count: 1`, and their old registrations are search terms.
- The successor was founded in the same month the old airline ceased, with a year-only date on a third aircraft that was delivered new.

The airlines, codes, registrations and serial numbers are made up and may coincide with real ones.
